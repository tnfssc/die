# Foundation review — 2026-09-25

**Verdict:** This is a useful isolated foundation, but not yet a sound foundation for enabling a real socket route. The tests exercise fakes, not the buffering and teardown behavior of actual provider and browser adapters.

- **High — Inbound audio has no aggregate backpressure** (`src/live/web-relay.ts:123–137`). Each frame is capped at 3,200 bytes, but an authenticated client can send frames arbitrarily fast. Every frame is base64-encoded and passed to `provider.sendAudio()` without a rate or provider-queue bound. The 12 KB limit at lines 58–67 covers only *outbound browser-socket* buffering. Add ingress pacing/byte budget and a bounded provider-send queue, with an explicit overflow outcome.
- **High — Closed sessions cannot recover from failed teardown** (`src/live/web-relay.ts:71–117, 283–285`; `web/live/controller.ts:189–226`). Both paths discard resource references even when `close()` or `stop()` throws. The relay then permanently returns `stopped: false` but cannot retry closure; the controller permits `start()` again from `error`, potentially opening another mic/socket while the old one remains live. Retain and retry failed releases, or prevent retry until teardown is verified.
- **Medium — Late-acquisition teardown failures are silent** (`web/live/controller.ts:87–90, 100–104, 118–120`). After `end()` invalidates a pending `acquire16k()` or `connect()`, a late `capture.stop()`/`transport.close()` rejection or throw is ignored by the `catch` because the generation is no longer active. An ended state can therefore claim success while a late microphone or socket survives. Report and handle late cleanup failure.
- **Medium — Session authority depends entirely on future wiring** (`src/live/web-relay.ts:27–48, 244–257`). The relay captures `SessionOperations` once and checks only its own `ended` flag before tool execution. It has no way to detect that the owning web session was revoked or switched while its socket remains open. The future route must bind a revocable owner capability and close the relay on ownership change; authentication at initial connection alone is insufficient.
- **Medium — Provider output/context queues are outside the stated bounds** (`src/live/web-relay.ts:163–183, 256–263`). Socket `bufferedAmount` bounds browser-bound audio, but `provider.sendAudio()` and repeated `provider.sendContext()` have no measurable upstream backlog. Real SDK buffering or rapid host updates can accumulate despite the relay’s bounded-frame tests. Verify the concrete provider’s queue behavior and apply a bound/coalescing policy before activation.

The absence of shipped route, UI, and device adapters is clearly documented; these findings concern behavior those adapters cannot safely fix merely by wiring them up.
