# Playback-time estimate for OpenAI Live accounting

Scope: the UI worker should pass `getPlayedAudioMs: () => playback.playedMs` (without the NUL markers) to its accounting callback. This change owns only the scheduler, offline tests and this note. No native, routing, waveform or speaker changes. No device/provider/key call was made.

`playedMs` is monotonic within a scheduler epoch and resets on `interrupt(epoch)`. It is an **estimate**, not a DAC acknowledgement or a count of generated/enqueued audio. A write only earns playback credit after its promise resolves; pending, failed, stale-epoch and pre-ready writes earn none. Elapsed clock time advances accepted PCM16/24kHz audio, capped by accepted duration and by the existing estimated native ring depth. A gap with no accepted samples does not accumulate time credit for later writes. Flush/interruption zeroes the epoch count; close freezes it. Late ring reports cannot retract previously returned progress. This does not alter frame pacing, the flush gate, queue/throttle, normal turn tails or waveform timing.

Native capability checked: `native/live/main.swift` emits `played(queuedMs)` from `ll_queued_ms`. `native/live/AudioCore.c` decrements `pending` when the render callback **pulls** samples. The helper sends an immediate queue snapshot on enqueue and a timer-based drain report with a 200ms zero grace; it does not acknowledge individual writes by epoch or confirm mixer/OS/DAC playback. `src/live/audio.ts` only forwards the queue-depth event; pipe write completion is not a native playback ack. Therefore this estimate may differ from what was physically heard (especially device stalls, buffering, output route changes, delayed/stale queue events or rejected native play commands). Do not present it as exact heard duration or charge based on it as proof of physical output. No native audio changes here.
