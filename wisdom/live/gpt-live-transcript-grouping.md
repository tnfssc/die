# GPT-Live provisional transcript grouping

GPT-Live `session.*_transcript.delta` gives timestamped provisional fragments, not completed ASR or reliable turn IDs. The transcript/history path now groups adjacent same-speaker fragments with at most 600 ms timestamp gap, 2500 ms span, 4096 characters, and 750 ms idle pause; a speaker or playback-suppression change also ends a group. Stop flushes remaining text. Entries stay `partial` (or output `suppressed`), including at an idle pause; timestamp boundaries are presentation boundaries, not finality. Arrival order is kept, including late corrections. The separate delegation bridge still sees each raw fragment and retains its existing offset/host authority checks; history grouping never authorizes agent work. The Gemini and OpenAI Realtime transcript paths and Mac capture/playback are unchanged.

Tests: `tests/live-transcript.test.ts` covers grouped speakers, gaps, size, span, idle; `tests/live-extension.test.ts` checks grouped provisional history alongside scoped delegation and suppressed output. See [GPT-Live delegation](gpt-live-delegation.md) and [transcript handoff gaps](transcript-handoff-gap.md). Values unchanged: existing truthfulness (#8), safe authority (#6), and bounded retention (#5) already cover this lesson.
